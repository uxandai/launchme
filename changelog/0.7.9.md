# launchme 0.7.9

Released 2026-11-09.

## New
- **Desktop window sidebar:** Library lists All games, Favorites, Hidden, then **Friends** (the friends list, with
  how many are online), **Profile**, **Downloads** (with how many are moving), **Settings** and **Store**. Profile,
  Settings, Downloads, Store, a game's details and *Change artwork* now open in the library's place beside the
  sidebar and top bar, which keep working; ← goes back.
- **Recently played** in the sidebar: the last three games with their covers, one click away.
- **Continue playing:** three wide tiles of your latest games above All games, with Play on hover.
- **List view** in the desktop window (the view menu → *List*): cover, name and state, platform, last played and
  play time.
- **Cogs on the sidebar's headings** (Library, Platforms, Emulation) open their place in Settings.
- **Emulation** is a Settings category of its own, under Library, in both modes.
- **The held game case in 3D** (Shelf and Cover Flow): its back and spine printed, and the game's manual (ES-DE's
  downloads or ScreenScraper) read like its booklet.
- **DRM-free and hand-added games are identified without a RAWG key:** by `steam_appid.txt`, GOG's game info or
  the Steam store search; store details show on the details page.

## Changes
- The desktop window's top bar keeps TV mode and "…"; Downloads, Settings and Store moved to the sidebar
  (Ctrl+J still opens Downloads).
- Platforms, Emulation and the groups below start folded in the sidebar; what you unfold is remembered.
- The section's heading shows its store's or console's logo.
- The game pane slides in and out; the "…" menu beside Play starts with *Details*, and *Details* is second in the
  right-click menu.
- Landscape cells keep the capsules' 920×430 proportions; the view button says which view is on when there is
  room; the list has no size slider.
- Settings → Library: *PC platforms* (Steam, the DRM-free catalogue, then the stores) with each platform's logo.
- Lifting a home row glows; rows can be dragged with the mouse in the desktop window.
- TV mode started with Windows takes the screen instead of starting minimized.
- The website moved to its own repository: https://uxandai.github.io/launchme-website/.
