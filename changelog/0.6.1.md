# launchme 0.6.1

Released 2026-11-03.

## Changed
- **Linux: Impeller is the only renderer; Skia is gone.** All drawing and text go through Impeller on Vulkan, else
  OpenGL (software Vulkan such as llvmpipe is skipped in favour of hardware OpenGL). Fonts use fontconfig and FreeType.
  Known gaps: DirectWrite character spacing is ignored on Linux and there is no CPU fallback without a working GPU
  driver. The `impeller` setting is removed.
- **Guide over a game: "Zamknij grę" is a menu like Zasilanie.** *Suspend the game* (only when quick resume is on and the
  game runs), *Close the game* (marked as dangerous) and *Cancel* (chosen first). A denied close or suspension keeps
  Guide open with its notification.
- **Guide's bar over a game is shorter**: Start (it was "Dashboard Launchme"), Close the game, Controllers, Sound,
  Widgets, Settings and Power. Search, All games, the store and Downloads are gone from it (they stay in the launcher).
- **Menus in Guide animate like in the launcher**: the highlight glides between the rows of the power menu, of the
  widget dialog and of the close menu, and the drawers move, instead of jumping.
- **Starting a game**: the background stays where it starts (no slow zoom), the "Uruchamianie…" text and the "Esc / B"
  hint are gone; the picture keeps the framing set in Kadrowanie (the same as the home screen's background); a thin ring with a white dot bouncing calmly off its edge shows that the game is starting. Back still
  hides the picture.
- **Statuses fade**: "W grze" and "Wstrzymana" on the tiles (shelves, Flix, Metro, XMB-style grids, coverflow, desktop
  grid) fade in and out instead of appearing and disappearing at once.

- **Controllers, Sound and Settings from Guide over a game**: leaving them (back on the home screen) returns to that game.

## Fixed
- **Empty library: Enter did nothing**; only Space pressed the focused button (*Add game*, *Platforms*, *Refresh*).
  Enter now presses it at once, as Space does (Windows and Linux).
- **Guide → Controllers / Sound / Settings could land on the home screen** when the library finished loading just
  after: the first load no longer takes the screen while a game runs or Guide opened it.
- **Companion with a Google (Gemini) key, HTTP 503**: Google answers 503 when a model is overloaded. After the retry,
  launchme now asks the other Flash models of the list in turn (never a Pro), and a remaining 503 is reported as an
  overloaded model, not a bare "HTTP 503".
