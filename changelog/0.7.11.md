# launchme 0.7.11

Released 2026-11-10.

## New
- **System logos in Settings → Emulation**: every system row shows its console's logo (PlayStation, Nintendo,
  Sega, Xbox, Atari, Commodore 64, ZX Spectrum, PC Engine, MAME, FBNeo, ScummVM and more); a system without one
  shows RetroArch's. The logos ship with launchme (`assets/systems`, sources in its README).
- **Friends as a page in the desktop window**: Friends in the sidebar is marked active and opens in the library's
  place, as Settings and Downloads do: the list on the left, the conversation beside it with the message field on
  the bottom edge, and Back as on the Profile page.

## Fixes
- Leaving Profile for another page in the desktop window flashed the profile over the whole window.
- Glance's section tabs in the desktop window cut their names short ("Your ga…").

## Changes
- With Glance on, the desktop window's sidebar lists it right under Store (it left the "…" menu).
