# launchme 0.7.22

Released 2026-11-18.

## Fixes
- TV mode at 4K no longer drops to 30–45 FPS while a tile without its own picture has the focus (XMB's *Recently
  played* clock tile and its categories, *All games*, Settings). The animated light ribbons behind those tiles cost
  about 8 ms per frame to draw; they are gone, and such tiles now show the plain background or your wallpaper.
