# launchme 0.7.25

Released 2026-11-24.

## New
- **Stage library view** (library → View options → *Stage*): the titles in a narrow column on the left, the
  focused game over the rest of the screen as on the PSfive home: its background, logo, a line of facts,
  *Play* / *Install* with the game menu, and badges for the platform, playtime and achievements.
  - *Short summary* (on by default): a sentence or two about the game between its logo and the buttons. PSfive
    has the same option (Settings and the theme studio).
  - *Dim background* (off by default): darkens the game's background in steps of 10%, up to 80%. One setting
    for every library shown as Stage.
- **Collections as tiles** (Grid → View options → *Collections as tiles*, off by default): your collections as
  a row of tiles above the games, in the grid's columns, with the shown games' count and order under them.
  - Each tile takes the grid's shape (squares, posters or landscape tiles) and the tile shape from Settings. It
    shows four covers, or one for a collection of a single game, with the name and game count under it.
  - Press Up from the first row to reach them, Left/Right to move, A to open a collection (Back returns to its
    tile). Left on the first tile opens View options. The collection tabs in the top bar give way to the tiles.
- **Grid columns** (Grid → View options → *Columns*): automatic, or 3–10 (2–6 for landscape tiles).
- **Filters** next to *Sort by*: *Installed only*, play modes (*Single-player*, *Multiplayer*, *Co-op*, *2+
  players on one screen*, *Online*, *Controller support*, VR) and *Never played*. Play modes come from store
  and RAWG tags; games without them match none. The sort button shows how many filters are on, and *Clear
  filters* turns them all off.
- **Interface sounds from UI SFX** (Settings → Sound → *Element change*: *Minimal (UI SFX)*, now the default):
  the Minimal pack (CC0) for moving the focus, opening menus and drawers, Back and starting a game. The earlier
  tone stays as *Synthesized*. *Notification* can use the pack's notification sound (silent by default).

## Changes
- **Edit metadata**: *Identify game* is the first button, in white, next to *Restore automatic*. It opens the
  databases' and stores' list in the same dialog: a database's game identifies it, a store's inserts the ticked
  details. Games added by hand and DRM-free games use the same list.
- **Change artwork**: the artwork in use shows first at once, marked *In use*; the rest loads behind
  placeholder tiles.
- **Manage Aurelia** in TV mode: a shorter introduction, and the same look and opening motion as the other
  dialogs.
- **Focus animation** (the springing outline) now also moves through Edit metadata, Change artwork, Manage
  Aurelia and the collection tiles.
- **Manually added games** (Settings → Library → DRM-free → *Manually added games*): *Add game* at the top of
  the list, as on the DRM-free page. In *Add game*, the focus outline is as round as the pill buttons.
- **Square covers** cut from posters or landscape art without readable lettering now keep their most detailed
  square (the logo and figures) instead of the middle. Crops saved by earlier versions are updated without
  reading the text again.
- The library's menus are tidier, and the short summary follows the summary language.
