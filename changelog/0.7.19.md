# launchme 0.7.19

Released 2026-11-17.

## New
- **Save resources behind a game** (Settings → System → Performance → *Save resources behind a game*, off by
  default): while a game runs full screen in front of the launcher, the launcher drops to 10 frames per second,
  lowers its CPU and memory priority and stops the video. Before, this always happened.

## Fixes
- The launcher no longer drops to 30 frames per second as soon as another window takes the focus (the
  notification area, a window beside it): a visible launcher keeps its full frame rate. It slows down only when
  minimized or hidden in the tray, or while idle as before.
