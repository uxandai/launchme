# launchme 0.7.8

Released 2026-11-07.

## New
- **Home screen and collections** (Settings → Library → *Home screen and collections*): one list of every home row,
  Recently played, PC platforms, Emulation, Publishers, Developers, the Steam rows and the collections, each with
  where it shows. A row's page sets *On the home screen* and *Tab in All games* separately, so a row can leave the
  home screen and stay a tab of All Games, plus that row's own options. A collection's page also has its games,
  its name, *Move to Steam*, *Delete* or the reset of its Steam assignments. *New collection* opens the games
  right after the name.
- **Rows in any order:** every row below Recently played moves, not only the collections. Hold A or Enter on a row
  (the hint bar says so), then ↑↓ carry it and A, Enter, Back or a click puts it down; the lifted row has a thick
  frame and a reorder mark. Shift+↑↓ and LB/RB still move the focused row, and a row's page has *Order* → *Move*.
- **View on a home row** opens that row's tab in All Games.
- **Steam friends through Aurelia:** when Aurelia reports every friend offline (its own presence is offline), their
  status comes from their public Steam profiles, so the friends list is no longer empty.

## Changes
- **Settings → Library** is reorganised: *Platforms*, then *Display* (*Home screen and collections*, *What to show*,
  *Library views*) and *Playing* (*Launching*, *Game saves*, *Shortcuts outside Launchme*). The old Collections and
  Sections pages are gone.
- **Platforms:** the stores that are off wait under *More platforms*; the ones you use stand at the top.
- **Identify game** is a button on the details screen only while the game is unidentified; after that it is in the
  "…" menu.
- **Settings → System → Help:** one *Walkthrough* row (the desktop window's first steps, TV mode's tour); the
  separate *Getting started* row is gone.
- The focus outline's *Pulsing* is stronger.

## Fixes
- **Launch animation:** it waits until the game's own window is in front; a store client's window (Steam, a
  launcher) no longer ends it early.
- **In-game overlay keyboard shortcut** opens the widgets, like the controller shortcut (it opened Home).
