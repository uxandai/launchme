# launchme 0.7.5

Released 2026-11-04.

## New
- **Desktop window: "Will it run?" check above the description.** A small bar in the details pane compares this PC
  (processor, graphics card, RAM, free disk space) with the game's minimum and recommended requirements: green (should
  run smoothly), yellow (should run, below recommended), red (may not run on this PC) or grey (no data, or the
  requirements could not be read). Click it to unfold the table *You have / Minimum / Recommended* per part. Steam and
  GOG games only; the requirements come from the store page (Steam's are cached with the game's metadata), the ratings
  from a built-in table of GPUs and CPUs. Green or yellow needs both the processor and the graphics card to be judged.
- **Snitch: the profile picture.** Up from the tile row focuses your Steam picture in the top-left corner (it grows
  and a light runs around its ring, like the bottom bar; the tiles lose their focus and outline). Enter, A or a click
  opens your profile with the achievements; Down, Right or Back returns to the row. The picture shows on the home
  screen too.
- **Settings → System → Discord:** opens the community server.

## Changes
- **Downloads:** the empty screen shows a cloud with a sad face and a centred message, in the same font as the other
  screens.
