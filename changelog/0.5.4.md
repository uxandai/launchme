# launchme 0.5.4

Released 2026-11-01.

## New
- **Category bar on Metro, Flix and PSfive** (Settings → Interface → TV mode → *Layout options* → *Category
  bar*, on by default): the library's category strip at the top in the middle of the home screen, three
  categories at a time as in the library (the first one at the left, later ones in the middle). LB/RB or Page
  Up/Down change the category, a click on a neighbour goes there. On Metro it takes the place of the
  lower-case tabs and on PSfive of its three tabs and *More...* (every category is on the strip); Up from the
  top row focuses it there. Switched off, the layouts' own tabs come back.
- **Shoulder hints beside every category bar**: small LB / RB key caps (L1 / R1, L / R, or PgUp / PgDn after
  the keyboard) on both sides of the strip, in the library too; dimmer when there is no category that way.
  They follow Settings → Interface → *Button hints*.
- **Hint style** (Settings → Interface → *Hint style*, *Glass* by default): the hints' key caps as light glass
  or white with dark lettering, everywhere.
- **PSfive start-up**: starting the launcher on PSfive plays the PS5 home's entrance: the bare background, then
  the row slides in quickly from the right, small, one tile after another; once it is in place the first tile
  grows to its focused size and the top bar, the logo, the buttons and the widgets fade in (about 1.7 s). Any
  move ends it where the tiles are; Settings → Interface → *Tile entrance* off skips it.

## Changes
- **Animated wallpaper under Settings** stands still: behind Settings the wallpaper is blurred, so its video
  pauses there (no decoding, no repaints) and plays on from the same frame afterwards.
- **Steam synchronization** (Settings → Library → Steam) tells why a change waits: Steam still loading its
  library, another account signed in, game privacy not loaded yet, or a Steam version that does not take the
  changes, instead of one "Steam has not confirmed the save" for all of them.

## Fixes
- **Hiding a game Steam does not know** (a removed demo or an ended playtest still in a Steam collection)
  no longer holds the Steam sync queue forever with "Steam has not confirmed the save": the change stays in
  launchme only and the bar says so; showing such a game again needs nothing from Steam.
- **Downloads in the Steam client with Aurelia on**: Aurelia's polls cleared the progress of every download it
  did not run itself, so a Steam download kept vanishing from Downloads and coming back (the screen flickered)
  and "Added to the download queue" appeared again and again. Aurelia now only touches its own transfers, and
  the notice comes once per download.
- **PSfive → Similar games** use each game's own crop (*Crop*, or the automatic one), as its tile does, instead
  of the centre of the cover.
