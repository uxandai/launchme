# launchme 0.7.0

Released 2026-11-03.

## New
- **Settings → Interface → Customize look (theme studio).** A full-screen layer like Settings: the back dot top left,
  tabs along the top (LB / RB: Layout, Wallpaper, Tiles, Colours, Fonts, Other, Looks), the options of the open tab on
  the left and the **real home screen, live,** on the right in the screen's own proportions. Every choice is a picture
  (layout thumbnails, tile shapes, entrance, focus gap, glow motion, grid patterns, fonts, button styles, colours) and
  is applied at once; switches and sliders glide, tabs slide, rows rise in. Lists that do not fit the column are a
  strip showing three and a half cards.
- **The preview shows what the focused option is about.** *Tile "Wszystkie" in rows* shows a collection's row, the
  home tiles show the end of the first row, Fonts show the library; changing the layout goes through black.
- **Idle demo.** After 1.5 s without input the preview walks along its row by itself (silent); any input stops it and
  puts the focus back where it was. The preview is never navigated by hand.
- **Looks.** The *Looks* tab saves the whole appearance under a name (`<config>\themes\<name>.ini`), loads it, deletes it
  (press twice) and *Undo changes* restores the look the studio was opened with.
- **Wallpaper** has a bin beside the picker (red under the pointer); the picker returns to the studio. *Grid pattern of
  the video background* gets a **None** card.

## Changes
- **Settings → Interface is short:** the few switches that matter for TV and desktop and the studio's door; the full
  list of appearance options is a page behind a tile.
- **Start-up focus.** The home always starts on its first tile (Ostatnio grane) instead of the game that last had the
  focus, however Recent is re-sorted while it loads.
- **Flix: the menu icons in the top right corner are gone,** with their setting; a saved `flix_top_icons` is ignored.
