# launchme 0.5.5

Released 2026-11-01.

## Changes
- **Long names glide instead of being cut off**: a name too long for its place fades out at the edge instead of
  ending in an ellipsis, and the focused one rests for 2 s, glides to its end, rests and glides back. In the
  category bars (the shown category's pill), the library's *List*, *List+* and *Detailed* rows, the *Grid*
  captions, *Cinematic* titles, *Overview*'s panel, *Shelf* and *Cover Flow* titles.
- **Downloads**: the progress bars glide to each new share the store reports instead of jumping from one
  sample to the next (TV mode's Downloads and the desktop window's).
