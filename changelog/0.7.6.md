# launchme 0.7.6

Released 2026-11-04.

## New
- **Identify game: one click.** The dialog starts looking at once: ScreenScraper first for an emulated game (when it is
  set up), then RAWG, and when no database knows the game, the best of the stores' descriptions (Nintendo eShop, Steam,
  GOG, Microsoft Store, TitleDB), which become its details. The best match is taken and its metadata and artwork are
  downloaded, with a spinner meanwhile. **Match by hand** (Enter, a click, or just start typing) shows the title field,
  a provider dropdown (all providers, RAWG, ScreenScraper, Nintendo eShop, Steam, GOG, Microsoft Store, TitleDB) and
  the list of matches; with all providers the stores' descriptions are listed below the databases' games, and a store
  you pick on its own gives its text details (no artwork).
- **Find in Edit metadata.** A button in the dialog's heading searches the stores and databases with the game's title
  (and a Switch title ID from its file name), lists what they found and copies the ticked details (description,
  developer, publisher, release date, genres) into the dialog; only Save makes them your own. Desktop window and TV mode.
- **Desktop window: "Getting started".** A card above the companion with a progress bar and the next step. Click it for
  the list: platforms and store accounts, service keys (with where to get them), the ROM folder, a first game, TV mode.
  Steps count as done when you take them or the library already shows it. Hide it from the list; Settings → System →
  Getting started brings it back from the first step.
- **Desktop window: the game panel** closes with its ✕ or by clicking an empty place in the grid, and comes back when
  you choose a game.
- **Desktop window: a view dropdown** next to the sort button: posters, squares or landscape cells.
- **Desktop window: the title bar keeps downloads, TV mode, settings and "…"**; Widgets, Glance, Tarot and the Store
  are in the "…" menu.
- **Add game (desktop window):** the file field is one line with its own picker; the folder button sits at the bottom
  as "Add folder as a library".
- **Switch 2:** `.nup` files are scanned and identified by the title ID in their name.

## Changes
- **A game's menu is grouped:** play, favourite, collections and details stay at the top; *Data and artwork*, *Game
  options* and *More* (folder, shortcuts, saves, plugin actions) are submenus; hide, exclude and remove stay at the end.
- **Hidden games:** in the desktop window a hidden game's menu says *Remove from hidden*. It also works for games
  Steam marks as hidden when launchme has no connection to Steam (private games stay private).
- **Dialogs and pages dim the window behind them** equally in the desktop window (Edit metadata, Details, Settings and the other pages used to be too light).
- **TV mode with a mouse:** right click on an empty place opens the bottom bar; on other screens it goes back.
- **Settings:** the library views of TV mode moved to *Library* (the *All appearance options* page is gone from
  *Interface*; the look of TV mode is in *Customize look*); *Library* has one *Launching and saves* section. In the
  desktop window *Interface* has a *TV mode look* row that takes you to TV mode, where *Customize look* has its live
  preview and the wallpaper.
- **Aurelia:** when Steam has not yet sent the friends' names and statuses, the widget asks again a few seconds later (up to three times) and says when it still has none.

## Fixes
- **Linux:** text cut with an ellipsis showed a broken character instead of "…" (the string was encoded twice).
