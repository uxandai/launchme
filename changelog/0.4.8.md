# launchme 0.4.8

Released 2026-10-31.

## Changes
- **Background cross-fade in TV mode**: moving between games about every half second no longer leaves two
  backgrounds showing through each other half-way. A new background waits until the focus stays on its game
  for a moment and its picture is ready, then cross-fades in 0.3 s; a cross-fade that started always finishes
  (or runs backwards when you go back to the previous game) before the next one begins. The two pictures are
  mixed without dimming in the middle and without the drifting echo copies. Cinematic's background does the same.
- **Logo rows on the default home**: the PC platforms, Emulation, Publishers and Developers rows no longer
  show the focused tile's name in large letters above the row; the tile's logo already says it.
- **Metro**: Publishers and Developers are logo pages like the PC platforms and Emulation: whole logos in wide
  tiles instead of cropped posters, and every one of them instead of the first twelve.
- **Publishers and developers on a dark tile**, as the PC stores, in every layout and on their board (console
  tiles keep their colours). A studio's library has no coloured glow behind it.

## Fixes
- Until a studio's logo badge loaded, the corner of the screen showed its key (`publisher:atari`) instead of its
  name.
