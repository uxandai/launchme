# launchme 0.7.18

Released 2026-11-17.

## New
- **PSfive game hub**: going down to *Play* lifts the whole home screen as on the PS5. The row leaves upward, the
  game's square cover and name settle in the top-left corner, the top bar fades out and the details' cards peek
  from the bottom. Down scrolls through the details one section at a time. Clicking a tile goes straight to *Play*.
- **PSfive details as activity cards** (Layout options → *Details under the row*): Achievements with a progress
  ring, Play time (hours, last played and HowLongToBeat's main story in one card), Description (Enter shows all of
  it) and Info, then the screenshots and similar games. The background blurs and zooms slightly behind them.
- **Music theme "Launchme — Quiet Exploration"** (Settings → *Music theme*).
- PSfive's top bar has Search and Settings icons.

## Changes
- The PSfive row keeps one and a half tiles in view on the left of the focused game; the first games stay at the
  left margin.
- TV mode Details uses the same cards as PSfive: four equal frosted cards (Achievements with its ring, Play time
  with the HowLongToBeat comparison, Description, Info), wider margins and larger screenshots. Down scrolls one
  section at a time over a blurred background. The Description card can be selected; Enter shows the whole text.
- The game menu ("…", a held Enter/A or a right-click) lists every option at once (Details, Remove from recently
  played, launch options, artwork, metadata, folder, shortcuts, hide, exclude, uninstall…) instead of *More
  options*, grouped by small gaps.
- The power menu has no dividers and rises in from below as it fades in, like the other menus.

## Fixes
- Local and plugin achievements (DRM-free games, other stores) now show in PSfive's details; only Steam's and the
  game index's counts appeared before.
- PSfive: no empty gap in the details when a game has no screenshots.
