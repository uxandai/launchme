# launchme 0.7.17

Released 2026-11-16.

## New
- **Backup and transfer** (Settings → System → *Backup and transfer*): export your profile to one
  `launchme_backup_YYYY-MM-DD--HH-MM.zip` and restore it here or on another PC, Windows or Linux. Tick the parts
  to bring over: settings, library (play time, collections, favourites, hidden games, games added by hand),
  chosen and downloaded artwork, API keys, store sessions, plugins, the companion. Keys, sessions, plugins and the
  companion are encrypted with a password. On restore, merge (the larger play time wins, collections are joined)
  or replace, and rewrite paths when drives differ (`D:\ROMs => /home/deck/ROMs`). A progress bar shows the
  work; the backup goes in at launchme's next start, and files it changes are kept in the backups folder.
- **Import from Playnite** (Settings → System → Backup and transfer → *Import from Playnite*): reads Playnite's
  library folder (found by itself on Windows) or a Playnite backup ZIP (also on Linux and the Steam Deck). Brings
  play time and last played, favourites, hidden games, categories, tags and completion status as collections,
  covers and backgrounds, games without a store and emulated ROMs; store sign-ins only when ticked. Nothing is
  removed.
- **Steam games' Proton settings on Linux** (a Steam game's menu → *Proton / Wine…*): its Proton version and
  Steam launch options, saved where Steam keeps them: at once while Steam runs with its CEF port, otherwise when
  Steam closes (each file backed up first).
- **GE-Proton updates itself** (Settings → Proton and Wine → *Update automatically*, on by default): once a day
  the newest GE-Proton is downloaded when you use GE-Proton; games on the automatic version use it at once.
- **Anti-cheat runtimes without Steam** (Linux): a game that needs Easy Anti-Cheat or BattlEye gets its runtime
  downloaded before it starts.

## Changes
- Desktop window: *Continue playing* tiles are now the first games of *All Games* instead of repeating below; they
  take the same selection outline and glow as the posters, and the arrow keys and pad move between them and the
  grid. The selection outline fades in and out.
- Desktop window: square tiles crop their covers around the subject, as TV mode does; *Continue playing* also
  shows when the library is sorted by last played.
- Desktop window: unfolded sidebar groups (Platforms, Collections, Recently played…) stay unfolded after a restart
  and after a trip to TV mode.
- TV mode: the mouse cursor hides after a few idle seconds.
- Uninstalling a Wine/Proton game can delete its prefix too (it warns when other games share it), and choosing a
  Wine build for a Proton prefix (or the other way round) warns first.

## Fixes
- Linux: while the Steam client runs, a pad no longer acts twice through Steam's desktop key layout.
