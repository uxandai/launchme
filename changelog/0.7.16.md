# launchme 0.7.16

Released 2026-11-16.

## New
- **Achievements without Galaxy** (Settings → Platforms → GOG, off by default): a GOG game started from launchme
  gets Comet, an open replacement for the GOG Galaxy service, with your linked account, so achievements, stats and
  leaderboards reach your GOG account without the Galaxy client. Downloaded once (about 14 MB).
- **Galaxy overlay** (Settings → Platforms → GOG, off by default, needs the option above): GOG's own overlay in
  GOG games (friends, chat, game invitations) on Shift+Tab. Downloaded once from GOG (about 100 MB). Windows, and
  Linux under Proton. While it runs, launchme's overlay stays on the Guide button.
- **Galaxy stand-in service** (Settings → Platforms → GOG): some GOG games (Cuphead, for one) talk to Comet only
  when the GOG Galaxy service exists; launchme can install an empty stand-in (Windows, asks for administrator
  rights) or add it to the game's Wine/Proton prefix (Linux).
- **Additional ROM folders** (Settings → Emulation → *Add a ROM folder*): more folders with system subfolders, on
  other drives say; the games from all of them join the library.
- **Handheld mode** (Settings → Interface → *Handheld mode*): TV mode on a small screen (Steam Deck, ROG Ally,
  Legion Go): text, covers and buttons a quarter larger, Metro's start page three columns wide. Switched on by
  itself at the first start on a Steam Deck, ROG Ally, Legion Go, MSI Claw, AYANEO, GPD, OneXPlayer or AYN.
- **Screenshots and similar games in details** (Settings → Interface, on by default): below a game's detail cards,
  its Steam store screenshots and similar games from your library, as PSfive's sheet has them. In TV mode press
  Down to scroll there; Enter shows a screenshot full screen (Left and Right for the others) or opens a similar
  game's details, Back returns to the buttons. In the desktop window they end the details page.

## Changes
- Library at small sizes: the tabs keep room for their names, the bottom row of covers fades under the button
  hints, and on Shelves the game's title hides while the quick bar is open.
- Linux: frosted glass panels blur the scene behind them in Impeller instead of drawing it again.
