# launchme 0.7.4

Released 2026-11-04.

## New
- **Snitch: Settings → Interface → Layout options → Coloured icons.** A few discs of the bottom bar take the real
  Switch's colours: search (red), library (blue), store (orange) and controllers (cyan); the rest stay grey. It is in
  the theme studio's Layout tab too, and saved with a look.

## Changes
- **Aurelia:** the note on its page now says in two paragraphs what Aurelia is for (managing Steam games without the
  Steam client: installs, updates, versions and branches, cloud saves, DLC, Workshop, chat) and when the Steam client
  may still be needed. The setting's row has a short description.

## Fixes
- **Play time of Steam games shows at once in the lists** (List, List+, the facts under the logo). It used to wait for
  the store page of each game, read one row at a time; now Steam's own count (`localconfig.vdf`, read once and shared)
  fills in until the page arrives.
