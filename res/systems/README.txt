System logos (white PNG) for Settings > Emulation.

Most are rasterized from the SVGs of ordovice/es-theme-logos (system_logos_bw/svg):
https://github.com/ordovice/es-theme-logos
That repository shares them for theme authors; it credits fabricecaruso's carbon and minimal themes.

psvita, ps4, ps5, switch, switch2, n3ds, xbox and xbox360 come from "Monochrome (Retro) Gaming Logos" by HVR88:
https://github.com/HVR88/Monochrome-Gaming-Logos
The author's usage note: the recreations are works of artistic expression, made under a fair-use principle
and provided as-is for identification purposes; use them freely and include a note and link back to the repository.

Launchme shows them only to identify the systems of the player's own emulators and games.
