Console case fronts

These PNG files (the "classic" frames: each console's own banner) come from "Game Cases" by
Twicethedad, a Decky Loader plugin: https://github.com/Twicethedad/Game-Cases
Launchme draws them over the cover art of Półka's cases (src/ui/package_renderer.cpp).
Changed here: switch.png has the logo re-centred on its red tile (the original's tile ran under the
rim on its left, cutting its margin).
